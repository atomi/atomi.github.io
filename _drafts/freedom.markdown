I've noticed a degradation in the societal norms of American culture since my youth. In large part because of fear terrorism has brought. I'm not even talking about things like the Patriot Act. I'm talking about everyday substantial differences in our culture and how we interact. Especially now with the political rhetoric.

We used to fight wars and lost countless lives. Now we cower when a few dozen people die and readily give up the very identity of our nation, and the very foundations that makes us exceptional. These are tragic deaths, but no where near the deaths we will lose fighting true fascism once it arrives. These may be trying times for our generation but my advice is to stop being afraid; be vigilant and steadfast in protecting our basic freedoms. That is all that matters.
