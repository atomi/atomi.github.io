 A lot these recipes are adapted from Peter Reinhart's recipes.  His bread recipes are unrivaled.   

 I consider most of these recipes to be fundamental in some way. Since I have made each of these at least a few times I have made some tweaks as necessary for my particular taste and flour differences. Your experience may be different..

Note: These recipes use [Baker's Percentage]; some math is required.
