---
title: Princess Faten Chilli Pepper
date: '2016-05-26'
---

This interesting chilli pepper I successfully propagated from seed might be related to the ornamental 'Flourescent Purple' (Capsicum annuum). They do look similar. The flowers are a bright violet and the leaves are dark green with blotches of black. The fruit is about ¼ to ½" in length and a very deep purple color nearly black. There is some minor damage from baby grasshoppers from earlier this growing season.  
Cool plant.
